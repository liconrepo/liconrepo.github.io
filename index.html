Mobile Design System

A small, opinionated, View-based design system for Android in Kotlin, with no Jetpack Compose dependency. Three tiers of tokens, one theme for many brands, light and dark from the same tokens, and accessibility as a default. Built to be read, copied and adapted. Contributions are welcome: fork the repo and open a pull request.
